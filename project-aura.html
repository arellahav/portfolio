AURA

UX/UI · Vibe Code
Year '26

Aura is a digital application designed to complement psychiatric treatment by providing real time distress support and clinical management between sessions. Developed using Vibe Code, the application enhances the therapeutic process through personal progress tracking and immediate assistance during moments of anxiety.

Challenge

Mental health care lacks continuity between scheduled sessions, leaving patients without immediate support during acute anxiety and without seamless progress tracking outside the clinic.

Solution

Aura bridges this gap by providing real-time distress support and continuous progress tracking, creating an accessible, all-in-one companion for both patients and clinicians.
