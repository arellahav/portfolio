We Didn't Know

Motion · Kinetic Typography
Year '26

A rhythmic kinetic typography project celebrating a powerful quote. By syncing a diverse mix of typefaces and bold colors directly to the audio, spoken words are transformed into a dynamic visual experience.

Challenge

Translating the cadence, tone, and emotion of a spoken voice into fluid visual motion using purely typographic elements.

Solution

Using audio-synced motion, shifting typefaces, and dynamic color to capture the natural rhythm of speech, turning every word into an expressive visual mark.
